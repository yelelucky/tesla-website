🧪 How do tests work? टेस्ट कैसे होते हैं?

Weekly tests, plus a test series through the year:

  • CWT: Chapter Wise Tests (early in the year)
  • PST: Part Syllabus Tests (mid-year)
  • FST: Full Syllabus Tests (after the syllabus is finished)
